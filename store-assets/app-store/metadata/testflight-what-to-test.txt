Please start with a fresh install or your existing save; tell us which you used. Do not delete an existing save just for this test.

1. First match: complete Learn the Basics, then play a Quick Match. Try passing, a held shot, a through ball, crossing, switching players, tackling and a timed skill move. Tell us which control or prompt was unclear.
2. Unlocks: score your first goal, return to the hub and open Road to Glory, Moments, Club Run and Blitz. Create a club and finish a league match. Check that coins, XP and the result persist after reopening.
3. Match recovery: switch to another app or lock the phone during play, then return. Check the score, clock, controls and sound. Close and reopen during a match and try Continue Match; report missing progress or duplicate rewards.
4. Tactics and menus: make a substitution at half-time, complete the match and open Shop > Store. Check scrolling, prices, wrapped text, touch targets and the screen edges on your device. Confirm accepted substitution prompts disappear.
5. Offline: enable Airplane Mode and play a Quick Match as a guest. Return online afterwards and check that local progress remains.
6. Optional services: if you use Apple sign-in, check cancel, sign-in, sign-out and returning to gameplay. If Store products are available, try a sandbox purchase and Restore Purchases. TestFlight purchases run in Apple's sandbox without charges. Report unavailable products or Game Center features as well.

This build uses test ads. If you choose a rewarded ad, check that it returns to the game and grants the displayed reward once. Report crashes, freezes, audio glitches, controls that stop responding or text you cannot read.

Use TestFlight > Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot and choose Share Beta Feedback. Include device model, iOS version, build number, game mode, what you expected and what happened. A short screen recording helps with timing or control issues.
