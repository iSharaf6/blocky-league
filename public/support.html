How do I play?

Pick LEARN THE BASICS on the main menu: three short drills get you going. On a phone or tablet, hold your thumb on the left of the screen to run. On the ball you have PASS, SHOOT, THROUGH, SPRINT and SKILL. Without it you have TACKLE, PRESS, SWITCH and SPRINT. Play with the phone on its side.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. On iPhone and iPad it lives inside the app and is included in your device backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so keep the app installed to keep your club.

The game feels slow

Open Settings and turn GRAPHICS down. Closing other apps helps on older devices too.

Privacy
