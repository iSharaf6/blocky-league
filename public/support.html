How do I play?

LEARN THE BASICS takes you through three short drills. On a phone or tablet, play in landscape and use the stick on the left to move. Push it farther to sprint. With the ball, use PASS, SHOOT and THROUGH; without it, use PRESS, TACKLE and SWITCH. The on-pitch trainer explains the controls as you play.

Where is my progress saved?

Your club is saved on your device. Optional sign-in backs up a copy to your private cloud account. Signing out keeps your local save. Deleting the app removes that local copy; sign in to the same account to recover a cloud save. Browser players can also export a save through Settings › Backup.

How do I continue a match?

Reopen the game and choose to resume the saved match. Local matches keep their score, clock, players and competition. Online browser friendlies cannot be resumed.

How do I restore a purchase?

On iPhone or iPad, open Shop › Store and choose RESTORE with the same Apple account. This restores eligible one-time purchases such as NO ADS and Coin Doubler. Spent gems are not restored. TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment; product availability can differ during the beta.

The game feels slow

Open Settings and turn GRAPHICS down. Closing other apps helps on older devices too.

Privacy

You can play without an account. Optional sign-in enables cloud saves and friend codes. The app's AdMob requests use family-rated, non-personalised ads; NO ADS removes the breaks between matches. Read the full privacy policy for details.
