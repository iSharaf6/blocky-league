Privacy Policy

Blocky League, by Calynx
Last updated: 3 October 2026

Blocky League is a football game for the web, iPhone and iPad. This page explains what information the game handles. The short version: you never need an account to play single-player. Optional sign-in, online friendlies, sharing clips and game-portal services handle the information described below.

The iPhone and iPad app

Your progress (coins, match record, clubs, badges and settings) is saved only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's own storage. We cannot see it. Your device's backup includes it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Ads. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you can choose to watch for coins, and now and then a full-screen ad between matches (never during play). Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so Google serves only family-rated ads, never personalised ones, and the app never asks to track you across other apps. To serve and measure ads, Google's software collects an approximate location from your IP address, a device identifier, the ads shown and how they were used, and crash and performance data. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google may ask for your consent first. Buying NO ADS removes the ads between matches.

Purchases. Coin packs, the Starter Pack and NO ADS are optional and paid through Apple. We receive only confirmation of what was bought, never your payment details.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the app reports your achievements to Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.

The app has no account of ours, no analytics of our own, no online play and no goal clips. It asks for no permissions (camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location). The sections below about sign-in, online friendlies, clips and portals apply to the web versions only.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, match record, chosen clubs, badges and settings such as sound, difficulty, graphics quality and your key bindings) is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key blocky-league-save-v1. It stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

When you play the standalone web version, your browser may also keep a cache of the game's files (a "service worker" cache) so it loads faster next time. That cache only contains game code, fonts and images.

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Doing so resets your progress. Settings › Backup lets you export your save as a file (and import it again); that file stays wherever you put it.

Goal clips

Where your browser supports it, the game keeps a short video of your latest goal in memory while you play, so you can save or share it. It is never uploaded: it only leaves the game when you press SAVE CLIP (a file on your device) or SHARE (your device's own share sheet, to the app you pick). It is gone when you leave the match.

Optional sign-in and cloud saves

Some builds offer an ACCOUNT button. Signing in is optional and only does one thing: it keeps a copy of your save in the cloud so you can carry it between devices. While you are signed in we store:

  • your save, as-is (coins, match record, settings, career and cup progress, level and challenges): one row per account, readable and writable only by you;
  • what the sign-in itself needs: for email, Google or GitHub sign-in, your email address, the provider's user id and the display name it sends, plus timestamps. Guest sign-ins keep none of that.

Cloud saves are private to your account and are not shared with opponents. Signing out keeps your local save; deleting your cloud save (in the account panel) or your account removes the stored copy.

Optional online friendlies

The standalone web and itch.io builds offer optional peer-to-peer matches through your browser's WebRTC data channels. When you host or join a match, your browser contacts Google's public STUN server to find a reachable network address. That service can see the connection's IP address. The connection codes you exchange with your friend contain connection details, and the connected opponent can learn network addresses and receives your selected club, match settings and game inputs. Your saved career, coins and account sign-in details are not sent to the opponent.

These matches use a direct browser-to-browser connection, with no game relay server, voice, camera or chat. The game does not request microphone or camera access. Online friendlies are excluded from the CrazyGames and Poki builds.

What we do not collect

  • No account is needed to play; there is no sign-up wall.
  • No contact-list or device-location permissions. Optional sign-in and online connections handle the details listed above.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts of our own.
  • No in-game chat.

Game portals and advertising

If you play Blocky League on a game portal (for example CrazyGames or Poki) or on a site that shows ads, that portal or ad provider runs its own software development kit (SDK) alongside the game in order to show ads (including optional "watch an ad for a reward" videos) and measure how the game performs. Those companies may use cookies or similar technologies and may process data such as your IP address, device and browser information, and ad interactions, as described in their privacy policies. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EU/UK), the portal or ad provider asks for it.

Please read the privacy policy of the site you are playing on. We do not receive personal data from these providers; portals may share aggregated, non-identifying statistics with us (such as play counts).

A portal may also back up your game progress to its own account system when automatic progress saving is enabled. That storage is governed by the portal's privacy policy.

Children

Single-player does not require personal information. Optional sign-in and online features handle the information described above. Ads and accounts on portals are controlled by the portal, which applies its own rules for younger players.
