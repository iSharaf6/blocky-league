# Tutorial repair and rebound-strike playtest — 2 October 2026

The previous readiness check verified tutorial actions, but did not prove that a beginner could complete the tutorial. The reported early PASS failure was reproduced from a fresh save using ordinary input.

## Cause and changes

The receiver in the original PASS setup was beyond the second-last opponent. A normal early pass triggered offside when he tried to receive it, rejecting possession and leaving the ball rolling past him. Waiting sometimes let the AI retreat into an onside position, which concealed the problem in earlier checks.

- The small teaching drills disable offside. Normal matches and Football Moments retain it.
- PASS runs in midfield and completes only when the intended teammate controls the pass. Scoring cannot bypass it; SHOOT has its own step.
- Players and the ball wait while a teaching cue is being read. The first action starts play immediately; a timed introductory banner no longer swallows that action.
- Practice has no hidden 30-second limit or half-time. Shooting and crossing use an open goal to teach the buttons before the first defended match.
- Lesson cues advance in order. A through-ball tap cannot bypass CROSS; that drill requires a lofted delivery before a goal. Guidance remains visible while the ball travels and after a miss.
- Basics use assisted controls without overwriting saved preferences. SPRINT can release its recovery cue; recovery timers stop during teaching holds.
- A queued first-time finish lasts through the predicted incoming ball arrival, bounded to 1.4–3.2 seconds. Previously a slower cross could outlive its queued shot.
- Phone landscape could hide the incoming CROSS instruction when the receiving player's head projected above the trainer's normal screen bounds. That left the ball held with no visible explanation. Lesson cards now stay on-screen, docking below the scoreboard when their player is outside those bounds.
- A secondary finger tapping PAUSE while the first held the stick did not generate the click the button relied on. Touch pause now responds to pointer-down and clears the stick and held buttons immediately.

## Rebound strike update

An early K tap on a rebound more than four metres from the player was previously discarded. K now queues a firm first-time shot on a reachable incoming loose ball. It reads the current flight immediately after keeper parries and post impacts, waits at most 2.35 seconds, and executes at contact without trapping the ball. Aim stays locked to the tap. Default movement assist takes a small step to meet the flight; manual steering remains in control. Opposition possession, another kick or expiry cancels the intent, and releasing a consumed K hold cannot fire a second shot. Defensive airborne clearances retain their existing control and no longer advertise the rebound-shot hint.

Actual contact produces the existing layered strike sound, ball flash and first-time trail, plus a two-frame impact hold and small camera shake for a firm human foot strike. There is no feedback or shot credit for an attempt that never meets the ball. Kick events carry the striker and first-time flag so a same-tick save/block cannot misattribute the shot.

- `reboundStrikes.test.ts`: 23 checks cover ground/bouncing arrivals, actual simulation-generated keeper parries and post collisions, aim, manual positioning, cancellation, expiry, auto-switch, consumed releases, targeted-pass compatibility and defensive clearance.
